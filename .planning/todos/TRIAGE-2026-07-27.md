## The finding that matters: three filed bugs were ported faithfully

The Python port carried three already-filed defects forward unchanged. Each was a
known, written-down bug at the time it was ported, and **the Phase 65 cutover does
not fix any of them.**

1. **MUSHY-9** — `\b[A-Z]{2,4}\b` at `gate/rules.py:24-25`, identical to
   `event-gate/rules.js:8-9`. `CA3` and `WEDGE` still rejected. Also duplicated in
   `gate/prompts.py:26,85` and, with different intent, `commit_harvest.py:36`.
2. **MUSHY-11** — `commit_observation.py:59` still returns
   `observation_requires_target`, and `tests/test_farmos_commit_simple.py:280`
   **pins it with a test**. This is the behaviour Don Santiago explicitly rejected
   (see `feedback_farmer_is_reality_source_of_truth`); a fix must now deliberately
   unpin a passing test.
3. **MUSHY-53** — the partial unique index at `persistence/migrations.py:184` is
   byte-identical in intent to `extraction-db.js:50-53`, so batch mode still drops
   every draft after the first on a multi-entry page.

This is the same shape as the Phase 64 parity-gate concern raised in the
2026-07-27 audit: **a faithful port of a bug passes a parity test.** Quirks 1-3 in
`63-07-SUMMARY.md` are pinned deliberately and documented as such; these three are
not — they are filed bugs that happened to be copied. The parity gate should not be
allowed to score them as matches without someone deciding that on purpose.
