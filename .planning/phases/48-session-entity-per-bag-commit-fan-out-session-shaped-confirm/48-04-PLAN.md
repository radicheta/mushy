Wire the Phase 45 ack contract end-to-end for `seeding_session` drafts. This plan extends both maps plus tunes the success-path body to include the session-shape disambiguator ("11 blocks across 5 parents") so the farmer sees confirmation of WHAT was committed, not just WHEN.
