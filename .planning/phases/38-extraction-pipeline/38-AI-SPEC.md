## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Structured Extraction (multimodal-aware, with conversational ask-back state machine)

**Description:**
Phase 38 extracts farmOS-schema-shaped JSON drafts from inbound multimodal Signal messages (free-text + Whisper-transcribed voice + photo attachments) sent by a small known set of mushroom farmers (3 pilot farmers in v1.7: Santi, Vikki, Selina). Phase 37 routes the message to the alerter; Phase 38 (this AI system) reads the capture row plus any in-flight draft from Timescale, calls Claude Sonnet 4.6 with `tool_use`-forced JSON output conforming to the locked B7 schema, and writes a draft row to `signal_draft` with per-field confidence. Phase 39 then renders the draft back to the farmer for confirmation; Phase 40 commits the confirmed draft to farmOS as a log (+ assets, where new). "Good" looks like: a multimodal inoculation message ("inoculated 5 shiitake bags today with the new sawdust mix, photo attached") produces a `seeding` log draft with `block_name` in B5 format (`YYMMDD_SPECIES3_SEQ`), correct `qty`, normalised `species`, and `_confidence` calibrated so that the genuinely uncertain fields trigger a targeted ask-back rather than a confident wrong guess. The system MUST prefer a one-line ask-back over a silent extraction error on ambiguous input.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. **Off-schema fields in a draft.** The LLM emits a key not present in the B7 `z.discriminatedUnion` (e.g. `mushroom_count`, a fabricated `pinning_date`, or a custom bundle). Violates EXT-01 (schema conformance) + farmOS convention C5 (native types only — no bespoke bundles).
2. **Confident wrong guess on ambiguous input.** Farmer says "the one we did last week" and the bot writes `block_name: 260504_SHI_03` with `_confidence: 0.9` instead of asking back. Violates NORTH-STAR (no silent data corruption of farmer records) and the calibration contract (high confidence → high precision).
3. **Multi-modal fusion failure.** A single message containing a voice note ("just inoculated five bags") plus a photo of those bags produces TWO drafts (one from transcript, one from image) instead of one fused draft. Violates EXT-03 SC#2.
4. **Lineage break on multi-parent events.** Farmer reports a harvest from blocks 3 + 4 + 5 of the same flush date; the extractor emits a single `parent_block_names: [<one block>]`. Violates EXT-05 + farmOS convention C4 (lineage must capture every source asset).
5. **Farmer-facing artifact corruption.** The ask-back reply to the farmer over Signal contains em-dashes (`—`), float-soup numbers (`94.3999%`), or LLM-tells. Violates memory `feedback_no_em_dashes_in_artifacts` and `feedback_round_farmer_numbers`. Note: em-dashes are fine in AI-SPEC.md and other dev artifacts; this failure mode is specifically about the bytes the farmer sees in Signal.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** Specialty mushroom cultivation (commercial small-farm scale) intersecting with agricultural records management / farm-operations bookkeeping. Not regulated food production at v1.7 scale; the system is the operational notebook for a working grow.

**User Population:** Three specific, named pilot farmers (Santi, Vikki, Selina — per memory `project_farmer_phone_map`). They are field-active practitioners: hands often dirty or gloved, frequently in cold rooms, grow tents, or in transit. Most messages arrive as voice notes dictated while moving substrate bags or doing other chamber work. Multilingual context: primary English with Spanish code-switching common; Whisper auto-detects per memory `project_farmer_language_and_gpu`. Farmers are paying close attention to the bot's output and treating it as a real operational tool, not a toy (memory `feedback_round_farmer_numbers`).

**Stakes Level:** **Medium.** Not life-critical and not externally regulated, but data corruption silently propagates downstream: a wrong-block-name draft confirmed by a distracted farmer becomes an orphan asset in farmOS, breaks lineage queries during contamination root-cause analysis, and skews yield-per-block math. Errors are recoverable but expensive (manual reconciliation) and erosive (farmer trust in the bot).

**Output Consequence:** Phase 38 drafts are not auto-committed; they feed Phase 39 (farmer confirmation in Signal) and then Phase 40 (farmOS write). However, the human in the loop is a busy farmer dictating from a cold room, so the confirmation step is realistically a "looks fine, send" tap. Once Phase 40 writes, the log + assets are persisted as farmOS records and drive: (a) yield-per-block reporting, (b) lineage walks during contamination triage, (c) harvest planning, (d) eventually inventory and customer-facing batch traceability. Wrong `species` → wrong yield expectations and wrong stage-of-life inference (via C1). Wrong `block_name` → orphan block, no lineage. Wrong `qty` or wrong units → bad yield/contam denominator.

        C4 lineage convention; downstream contamination triage depends on

Dimension: Quantity literalism
Good: "3 bags of about 250 grams each" → qty=3, weight_per_unit_g=250 (with
      lowered confidence on the ~250). Bot does NOT compute 750g total. "A

Bad:  Bot multiplies units silently (qty=750). Bot converts farmer's units

      "5" to "about 5" or vice versa.
Stakes: High
Source: Practitioner expectation — yield math must use the farmer's literal
        unit; downstream aggregation is farmOS's job, not the extractor's.
        Memory: feedback_round_farmer_numbers.

Dimension: Confidence calibration
Good: When the LLM emits _confidence[field]=0.9, that field is correct

Bad:  Uniformly high confidence regardless of input quality (the "0.95 on

Stakes: Critical

Good: Stage information (pinning, fruiting, exhausted) is encoded as an

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

- **Handwriting OCR drift on paper-log photos.** mushdatadump's 73 JPEGs are photographs of handwritten farmer notebooks; image quality varies. SEQ digits especially tend to drift (a smudged "3" reads as "8").
- **Spanish-English code-switch in voice notes.** Whisper output frequently mixes languages within a single transcript ("inoculé five bags de shiitake"). Species names appear in informal Spanish forms ("gírgolas", "shitake" without h, "melena de león") that need normalisation to canonical species.
- **Multiple events in one voice note.** Farmer dictates "inoculated 5 blocks today AND tagged the bags from last week's harvest" in a single message. Extractor must either split into two drafts or NOT silently merge them into one log.
- **Date ambiguity from natural language.** "Yesterday", "this morning", "last week" all need timestamp resolution against the capture row's `received_at`. Ambiguous dates should lower confidence on `event_timestamp`, not be confidently resolved.
- **QR codes partially visible in frame.** Photos sometimes contain a block's QR sticker. Phase 38 does NOT do QR decoding (that's Phase 40), but the presence of a QR is a strong signal about block identity and should not be silently ignored — the LLM should at least caption "QR sticker visible" so Phase 40 can act on it.
- **Substrate naming variance.** Farmer writes "sawdust mix", "supplemented sawdust", "S+B" (sawdust + bran), "MSS" (master's sawdust supplement). Schema field is free text per B5; the failure mode is over-normalisation (rewriting the farmer's literal phrase) rather than under-normalisation.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

**None identified for v1.7.** Private farm bookkeeping at small commercial scale; no FDA / USDA / Uruguayan MGAP reporting requirements at this volume; farmOS is the system of record but is not externally audited. The locked B7 schema is the only compliance surface, and it is self-imposed.

For the record: future commercial expansion (export, retail labelling, food-safety certification) would intersect with FDA FSMA traceability rules (lot-level lineage, electronic records integrity) and Uruguayan MGAP food-traceability requirements. Out of scope for v1.7; the B7 schema's strict C4 lineage discipline is forward-compatible with FSMA-style audits if/when that day comes.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Raw `@anthropic-ai/sdk` (already installed, Phase 25) with Anthropic **tool-use** for JSON-mode enforcement + **Zod** for schema definition & runtime validation.

**Version:**
- `@anthropic-ai/sdk` ^0.91.1 (locked, existing)
- `zod` ^3.x (new dependency)
- Model: `claude-sonnet-4-6` (locked in `src/agents/alerter/src/llm-client.js`)

**Rationale:**
The alerter already has a battle-tested Anthropic Sonnet 4.6 client (`llm-client.js`) with retry, degraded path, and structured logging — every JS LLM orchestration framework (LangChain.js, Vercel AI SDK, Mastra, Inngest AgentKit, Claude Agent SDK) would replace working code with a thicker abstraction without solving Phase 38's actual hard problems. Phase 38's hard problems are (a) **B7-schema-conformant output**, solved by Anthropic's native tool-use (define a `submit_extraction` tool whose `input_schema` is the JSON Schema generated from Zod — the LLM is forced to call the tool with arguments matching the schema), and (b) a **DB-backed 3-turn ask-back state machine**, which lives in Timescale rows per D-02, not in framework memory. Zod is the JS-idiomatic schema layer that gives us **one source of truth** that (1) feeds Anthropic's `input_schema`, (2) runtime-validates the LLM's response before insert into `signal_draft`, (3) provides compile-time types in the alerter, and (4) is reused by the offline eval harness deterministic schema-validator step (D-07).

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| LangChain.js / LangGraph.js | JS parity adds abstraction overhead; supersedes already-debugged `llm-client.js` retry path; extraction with one LLM and a DB-backed state machine is the Anti-pattern #1 case ("LangChain for simple chatbots — direct SDK is less code") |
| Claude Agent SDK (TS) | Tool-use/MCP/filesystem-oriented for autonomous code agents; ask-back UX + Timescale draft persistence don't map onto its hook model |
| CrewAI / LlamaIndex / Haystack / Google ADK / AutoGen | Python-only or wrong domain; alerter is Node.js |
| Vercel AI SDK / Mastra / Inngest AgentKit | JS-native, but introduce new abstractions over the SDK call we already own — solve no problem we have |
| OpenAI Agents SDK | Wrong provider (locked on Anthropic Sonnet 4.6) |
| `ajv` + hand-written JSON Schema (no Zod) | **Alternative kept in reserve.** Fallback if Zod's `z.discriminatedUnion` (needed for B7 log-type discrimination) doesn't round-trip cleanly with Anthropic's accepted `input_schema` subset. Drop to hand-authored schema + ajv keeps validation deterministic without fighting an emitter. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial. Locked on Anthropic (claude-sonnet-4-6) per upstream Phase 25 decision — model-provider swap would touch `llm-client.js` but not the extraction logic. Zod is provider-agnostic; the JSON Schema it emits can feed OpenAI's structured outputs, Gemini's function calling, etc. if we ever swap. No new infrastructure lock-in.

### Entry Point Pattern
```js
// src/agents/alerter/src/extraction/extractor.js
// Minimal working extraction call — Zod schema → JSON Schema → Anthropic tool → tool_use → validate.

const Anthropic = require('@anthropic-ai/sdk');
const { z } = require('zod');
const { zodToJsonSchema } = require('zod-to-json-schema');

// 1) Zod schema — one source of truth for shape + runtime validation.
const SeedingLog = z.object({
  log_type: z.literal('seeding'),
  species: z.string().describe('mushroom species, e.g. shiitake'),
  block_name: z.string().describe('B5 format: YYMMDD_SPECIES3_SEQ'),
  qty: z.number().int().positive(),
  event_timestamp: z.string().datetime(),
  substrate: z.string().optional(),
  // Per-field confidence (D-03 ask-back trigger).
  _confidence: z.record(z.string(), z.number().min(0).max(1)),
});

// 2) Emit JSON Schema draft-7 for Anthropic's tool.input_schema.
const inputSchema = zodToJsonSchema(SeedingLog, { target: 'jsonSchema7', $refStrategy: 'none' });

// 3) Anthropic call — force the model to call our extraction tool.
const client = new Anthropic({ apiKey: process.env.ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, maxRetries: 2, timeout: 60_000 });

async function extract({ systemPrompt, userBlocks }) {
  const msg = await client.messages.create({
    model: 'claude-sonnet-4-6',
    max_tokens: 1500,
    temperature: 0,
    system: systemPrompt,
    tools: [{
      name: 'submit_extraction',
      description: 'Emit the structured farmOS draft for this farmer message.',
      input_schema: inputSchema,
    }],
    tool_choice: { type: 'tool', name: 'submit_extraction' },
    messages: [{ role: 'user', content: userBlocks }], // userBlocks may include text + image blocks
  });

  // 4) tool_choice forces a tool_use block — NOT a text block.
  const toolUse = msg.content.find((b) => b.type === 'tool_use' && b.name === 'submit_extraction');
  if (!toolUse) return { ok: false, reason: 'no tool_use block' };

  // 5) Zod runtime-validates what the LLM emitted. Anthropic's input_schema is a hint, not a hard guarantee.
  const parsed = SeedingLog.safeParse(toolUse.input);
  if (!parsed.success) return { ok: false, reason: 'zod_invalid', issues: parsed.error.issues, raw: toolUse.input };
  return { ok: true, draft: parsed.data, tool_use_id: toolUse.id };
}

module.exports = { extract, SeedingLog };
```

### Key Abstractions

| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|------------|-----------------|
| Anthropic tool-use | LLM is shown one or more `tools` with JSON-Schema `input_schema`; response is a `tool_use` content block whose `input` is JSON conforming to the schema. | The whole extraction path — every Phase 38 extraction is a forced tool call. |
| `tool_choice: { type: 'tool', name: '...' }` | Forces the LLM to call a named tool (vs. `auto` / `any`). Response is guaranteed to contain that `tool_use` block. | Extraction call. The continuity-decision call can use `tool_choice: 'any'` if multiple tools are offered. |
| Multimodal `content` blocks | A user message's `content` can be an array of `{ type: 'text' }`, `{ type: 'image', source: { type: 'base64', media_type, data } }`, etc. Up to 5MB / image; up to ~20 images per request. | When a Signal photo attachment path is on disk — read, base64-encode, attach. |
| `tool_result` content block | Multi-turn pattern: after a `tool_use`, the next user message includes `{ type: 'tool_result', tool_use_id, content }` to feed validation errors back. | Retry on Zod validation failure (Section 4b); ask-back state machine. |
| `z.discriminatedUnion` | Zod combinator for tagged unions on a literal key. Emits an `oneOf` in JSON Schema. | B7 log_type union (seeding | activity | input | observation | harvest) — single tool, polymorphic payload. |
| `zod-to-json-schema` | Emits JSON-Schema draft-7 (or 2019-09) from a Zod schema. Target `jsonSchema7` + `$refStrategy: 'none'` produces the inlined form Anthropic accepts. | Wherever a Zod schema must reach Anthropic's `input_schema`. |
| `.parse` vs `.safeParse` | `.parse` throws on failure; `.safeParse` returns `{ success, data | error }`. | Always use `.safeParse` at the LLM boundary — we surface the error to the retry path, never let it throw. |

### Common Pitfalls

1. **Anthropic accepts only a SUBSET of JSON-Schema draft-7.** `$ref`, `allOf`, many `format` keywords, and some `oneOf` shapes are rejected at request time with a 400. Mitigation: pass `{ target: 'jsonSchema7', $refStrategy: 'none' }` to `zodToJsonSchema` to inline everything; in CI assert the emitted schema parses with Anthropic's validation by making a `messages.count_tokens` dry-call in a unit test. Fallback documented in Section 2: drop to a hand-authored JSON Schema + `ajv` if `z.discriminatedUnion` doesn't round-trip cleanly.
2. **`tool_choice={type:'tool'}` returns a `tool_use` block, not a `text` block.** The existing `llm-client.js` reads `msg.content[0].text` — extraction code must instead `find(b => b.type === 'tool_use')`. There may also be a leading `text` block with the model's "thinking out loud" — never assume index `[0]`.
3. **Image content blocks must be base64-encoded inline (not file paths) OR a URL Anthropic can fetch.** Signal saves attachments to disk paths (Phase 25 `attachment_paths text[]`). Read the file, base64-encode, attach as `{ type: 'image', source: { type: 'base64', media_type: 'image/jpeg', data } }`. Cap: 5MB per image, downscale large photos before encoding.
4. **Token budget for inline images is non-trivial.** A single 1024×1024 JPEG is ~1600 tokens; a phone photo at 4032×3024 is ~3500 tokens *plus* the base64 payload itself. Phase 38 prefers (a) the whisper transcript already produced by Phase 25, (b) a downscaled photo (max 1568 long-edge per Anthropic's recommendation), (c) the raw text.
5. **Zod schema descriptions become part of the JSON-Schema the LLM sees.** Use `.describe('B5 format: YYMMDD_SPECIES3_SEQ')` aggressively — this is the cheapest way to teach the model the B7 conventions inline. Don't repeat the same explanations in the system prompt.
6. **`@anthropic-ai/sdk` exports CommonJS-default differently across versions.** The existing `llm-client.js` uses `const Anthropic = require('@anthropic-ai/sdk')` and `new Anthropic({...})`. On the installed v^0.91.1 this works; do NOT switch to `const { Anthropic } = require(...)` without verifying — the default export shape is the constructor.
7. **Whisper transcripts already exist before the extractor runs.** Phase 25's `transcribe-client.js` populates `signal_capture.transcript`. The extractor MUST read from DB, not re-transcribe — don't reinvent the audio path.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**
- **Extraction call:** `claude-sonnet-4-6`, `temperature: 0`, `max_tokens: 1500`, `tool_choice: { type: 'tool', name: 'submit_extraction' }`. Deterministic for replay-safety (D-02a).
- **Continuity decision call (D-01):** same model, `temperature: 0.3`, `max_tokens: 300`, `tool_choice: { type: 'tool', name: 'decide_continuity' }` with three-way enum `append | replace | start-new`. Slight temperature is fine — the decision is logged and replay-safe via the 30-min idle cap (D-01a).
- **Ask-back composition call:** same model, `temperature: 0.4`, `max_tokens: 200`. Generates the farmer-facing Signal reply (the `[?]`-annotated preview + one-line top question, D-04). Outputs plain text, not tool-use.
- **Client:** `new Anthropic({ apiKey, maxRetries: 2, timeout: 60_000 })` — matches the existing `llm-client.js` retry posture; adds an explicit 60s timeout (the SDK's default is much longer and would stall the alerter's WS loop).

**Core Pattern:**

Two LLM calls per inbound farmer message, both stateless — all conversation state lives in Timescale (`signal_capture` + `signal_draft`).

```js
// extraction/extractor.js — orchestration sketch
async function handleInboundCapture(captureRow) {
  const inFlight = await draftDb.findInFlight(captureRow.sender_e164);

  // Phase A — continuity (only if a draft is in flight)
  let action = 'start-new';
  if (inFlight && (Date.now() - inFlight.last_touched_ms) < THIRTY_MIN_MS) {
    const decision = await continuity.decide({ draft: inFlight, capture: captureRow });
    action = decision.action; // 'append' | 'replace' | 'start-new'
    await draftDb.logContinuity(inFlight.id, captureRow.id, decision);
  }

  // Phase B — extraction
  const draftCtx = action === 'append' ? inFlight : null;
  if (action === 'start-new' && inFlight) await draftDb.expire(inFlight.id);

  const userBlocks = await multimodal.buildUserBlocks({ captureRow, draftCtx }); // text + image + transcript
  const result = await extract({ systemPrompt: SYSTEM, userBlocks, draftCtx });

  if (!result.ok) {
    // Retry once with the validation error fed back via tool_result (Section 4b).
    return await extractWithRetry({ userBlocks, draftCtx, prior: result });
  }

  // Per-field confidence check → D-03 ask-back trigger.
  const lowConf = Object.entries(result.draft._confidence).filter(([, v]) => v < THRESHOLD);
  const status = lowConf.length || hasUnresolvedRequired(result.draft) ? 'awaiting_farmer' : 'pending';

  await draftDb.upsert({ ...result.draft, status, source_capture_ids: [...(draftCtx?.source_capture_ids || []), captureRow.id] });
  if (status === 'awaiting_farmer') await askBack.compose({ draft: result.draft, lowConf });
}
```

**Tool Use:**
- **`submit_extraction`** — primary tool. `input_schema` is the JSON-Schema emitted from the B7 `z.discriminatedUnion('log_type', [...])`. Includes a top-level `_confidence: Record<string, number>` mirroring each emitted field (per D-03). The LLM is told in the system prompt: *"When you are unsure of a value, emit your best guess AND lower its confidence — do not refuse to fill the field."*
- **`decide_continuity`** — separate tool for Phase A. `input_schema` is a tiny `{ action: enum, rationale: string }`. Keeps the two calls' contracts isolated so a continuity decision can never accidentally write a draft.
- **No `request_clarification` tool.** Simpler to keep ask-back deterministic: the LLM emits per-field confidence, the post-validator decides whether to ask back, and the ask-back composition is a third (text-only) call. This avoids the failure mode where the LLM oscillates between extracting and asking.

**State Management:**
- **Source of truth:** Timescale tables `signal_capture` (Phase 25, existing) and `signal_draft` (new, D-02). LLM holds no state across calls.
- **Per-sender invariant (D-02c):** at most one in-flight draft per sender E.164. Enforced by partial-unique index on `signal_draft (sender_e164) WHERE status IN ('pending','awaiting_farmer')`.
- **Status transitions Phase 38 owns:** `pending → awaiting_farmer → (needs_review | expired)`. Phase 38 also sets `pending` directly to `awaiting_farmer` when confidence < threshold from the first turn.
- **Ask-back turn counter:** `signal_draft.ask_back_turns int` increments on each composed ask-back; on `>= 3` (D-05), status → `needs_review` and the bot sends the round-numbered "I can't lock this one, marked for manual review" reply.
- **Replay safety:** `signal_draft.id` is `sha256(sort(source_capture_ids).join(','))` (D-02a). Replaying the same capture sequence produces the same draft id — `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (id) DO UPDATE` is idempotent.

**Context Window Strategy:**
- Claude Sonnet 4.6 ships with a 200K context window. Phase 38's per-call budget is far below that; the discipline is about **latency and cost**, not the model's ceiling.
- **Per extraction call:** system 1.5K + few-shot 3K + draft summary 0.5K + new text/transcript 1–2K + 1 downscaled image (~1.6K tokens) + Whisper transcript text ≈ **~8–10K input**, ≤1.5K output.
- **Multi-turn ask-back compaction:** each ask-back call sends ONLY (a) the static system + few-shot block (cached, see 4b cost), (b) a compact draft summary (the `[?]`-annotated render the farmer last saw, ~300 tokens), and (c) the farmer's most recent reply. Do NOT replay the full capture history — it is already encoded in the draft, and replaying invites the model to relitigate already-locked fields.
- **Image inclusion rule:** include the photo on the FIRST extraction turn only. On ask-back turns the photo is summarised as `"original photo: <one-line LLM-emitted caption from turn 1>"` to save tokens — the farmer's ask-back replies are text/voice, not new photos in the common case.
- **Past drafts not included.** Cross-draft context (e.g. "what species did farmer 1 inoculate last week") is out of scope for Phase 38 extraction — that's a Phase 40+ retrieval concern.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### Structured Outputs with Zod (JS/TS equivalent of Pydantic)

The Phase 38 contract is: **a Zod schema is the single source of truth.** It feeds (1) Anthropic's `input_schema` via `zod-to-json-schema`, (2) runtime validation of the LLM's `tool_use.input`, (3) the eval harness's deterministic schema-validator step (D-07), and (4) the alerter's compile-time types (if/when this file converts to `.ts`).

```js
// src/agents/alerter/src/extraction/schemas/index.js
const { z } = require('zod');

// Per-field confidence wrapper — mirrors the emitted field set; LLM fills 0..1 per key.
const Confidence = z.record(z.string(), z.number().min(0).max(1));

const SeedingLog = z.object({
  log_type: z.literal('seeding'),
  species: z.string().min(2).describe('common name, e.g. shiitake'),
  block_name: z.string().regex(/^\d{6}_[A-Z]{3}_\d{2,4}$/).describe('B5: YYMMDD_SPECIES3_SEQ'),
  qty: z.number().int().positive(),
  event_timestamp: z.string().datetime(),
  substrate: z.string().optional(),
  _confidence: Confidence,
});

const HarvestLog = z.object({
  log_type: z.literal('harvest'),
  parent_block_names: z.array(z.string()).min(1).describe('C4 lineage; one or more parent blocks'),
  qty_grams: z.number().positive(),
  event_timestamp: z.string().datetime(),
  grade: z.enum(['A', 'B', 'discard']).optional(),
  _confidence: Confidence,
});

// ... activity, input, observation similarly.

const Draft = z.discriminatedUnion('log_type', [SeedingLog, HarvestLog, /* ... */]);

module.exports = { Draft, SeedingLog, HarvestLog };
```

**Plugging into Anthropic's tool:**

```js
const { zodToJsonSchema } = require('zod-to-json-schema');
const inputSchema = zodToJsonSchema(Draft, { target: 'jsonSchema7', $refStrategy: 'none' });
// inputSchema is now a draft-7 oneOf — pass it as tools[0].input_schema.
```

**Retry logic on validation failure** (single retry, then degrade):

```js
async function extractWithRetry({ systemPrompt, userBlocks, priorTry }) {
  // First call already returned ok:false with { reason: 'zod_invalid', issues, raw }.
  // Feed the validation error back via a tool_result content block.
  const repairMessages = [
    { role: 'user', content: userBlocks },
    { role: 'assistant', content: [{ type: 'tool_use', id: priorTry.tool_use_id, name: 'submit_extraction', input: priorTry.raw }] },
    { role: 'user', content: [{
      type: 'tool_result',
      tool_use_id: priorTry.tool_use_id,
      is_error: true,
      content: `Your previous output failed schema validation. Issues: ${JSON.stringify(priorTry.issues)}. Re-emit submit_extraction with corrections.`,
    }] },
  ];
  const msg = await client.messages.create({ /* ...same params... */, messages: repairMessages });
  // Validate again; on second failure, log + mark draft needs_review (do NOT loop further).
}
```

Retries: **1 maximum.** Two consecutive Zod failures imply a schema/prompt mismatch the planner needs to see — surface the raw `tool_use.input` + Zod issues to `signal_draft.audit_jsonb` and transition to `needs_review`.

### Async-First Design

- The `@anthropic-ai/sdk` is Promise-native; every call is `await client.messages.create(...)`. The alerter's outer loop (Phase 17/25) is already an `async` WS handler — no new async machinery needed.
- **The one common mistake:** not setting `timeout` and `maxRetries` on the client. The SDK's default retry behaviour exponentially backs off and can stall the alerter's WS consumer past the message-ack deadline. The existing `llm-client.js` sets `maxRetries: 2`; the extractor adds `timeout: 60_000`. Both are mandatory.
- **Stream vs await:** the **extraction call** is awaited end-to-end — streaming gains nothing (we cannot validate a partial `tool_use.input` against Zod). The **ask-back composition call** (text output) MAY stream if Phase 39 wants typing-indicator UX in Signal; default Phase 38 implementation awaits because Signal delivery is one-shot anyway.
- Run continuity + extraction **sequentially**, not in parallel — the extraction call's `draftCtx` depends on the continuity decision (`append` vs `start-new`).

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

**System prompt (static, cacheable — see Cost section):**
- Role: *"You extract structured farmOS log/asset drafts from a Uruguayan mushroom farmer's Signal messages."*
- B7 schema explanation: condensed map of the five log types + the four asset types + the B5 block-name convention + the C4 lineage rule. ~800 tokens.
- Extraction principles: *"When uncertain, emit your best guess and lower the field's `_confidence` below 0.7 — do NOT refuse to fill a field. Never invent timestamps; if none is given, use `now` and confidence 0.5."*
- Farmer-language conventions: *"Farmer writes in Spanish/English; species names may be informal ('shitake', 'gírgolas'); normalise to canonical species."*

**User prompt (per-message, dynamic):**
- Header: `Inbound message from {farmer_slug} at {iso_ts}`
- Block 1: raw text (if any)
- Block 2: Whisper transcript (if any) — labelled `[voice transcript]`
- Block 3: image content block(s) — labelled `[photo 1 of N]`
- Block 4: if `draftCtx` present, `[in-flight draft summary]` + the `[?]`-annotated preview the farmer last saw
- Block 5: explicit instruction *"Call submit_extraction now."*

**Few-shot:** 3–4 worked examples drawn from `mushdatadump/` ground truth, hand-picked to cover: (a) pure-text inoculation, (b) text + photo harvest, (c) voice-only with B5 block name, (d) multi-block harvest with lineage. Pinned in `prompts/few-shot.js`; held flat in the system block to maximise cache hits.

**Token budget per extraction call:** system 1.5K + few-shot 3K + user 1–2K + 1 image 1.6K + output ≤1.5K = **~9K total**. Hard cap `max_tokens: 1500` on output is non-negotiable — leaving it unbounded is the production foot-gun.

### Context Window Management

- **No cross-draft retrieval.** Each draft is self-contained; the Phase 38 extractor never reads other drafts. (Phase 40 may; not our problem.)
- **Ask-back compaction:** each ask-back turn includes the static system + few-shot (cached), a 300-token `[?]`-annotated draft snapshot, and ONLY the farmer's most recent reply. Capture history older than the current draft's `source_capture_ids` is never replayed.
- **Image carry-over:** photo attached on turn 1 only. On ask-back turns it is replaced with a one-line caption emitted by the LLM on turn 1 (cached in `signal_draft.image_caption`).
- **30-min idle cap (D-01a)** is the safety net: anything older than 30min forces `start-new`, so the longest possible context is one draft × ≤3 ask-back turns. No conversation can grow unbounded.

### Cost and Latency Budget

- **Pricing (Claude Sonnet 4.6, current):** ~$3 / MTok input, ~$15 / MTok output. With prompt caching: cached input reads at ~$0.30 / MTok (10× cheaper).
- **Per-draft estimate (uncached):** 10K input + 1.5K output ≈ **$0.053 / draft**. With 3 ask-back turns: ~$0.10–0.12 / draft worst case.
- **Per-draft estimate (cached system + few-shot):** the 4.5K static block reads at cached rate, fresh input ~3K. ≈ **$0.025–0.03 / draft**, ~50% saving.
- **Prompt caching strategy:** mark the system prompt + few-shot block with `cache_control: { type: 'ephemeral' }` (Anthropic 5-min ephemeral cache). The cache key is the exact byte content — keep `prompts/system.js` + `prompts/few-shot.js` static; never interpolate per-message data into either. Verify cache-hit rate via `usage.cache_read_input_tokens` on each response.
- **Mushdatadump eval cost (D-07 ship-gate):** 73 cases × avg ~2 turns × ~$0.03 cached ≈ **~$5 per full eval run**. Cheap enough to run on every Phase 38 PR. Uncached worst case ~$15.
- **Latency targets:** continuity decision ≤2s (small payload, small max_tokens), extraction call 5–8s (image + larger output), ask-back composition ≤2s. Farmer-perceived: ≤10s from Signal-message-receive to bot-reply on extraction turns. Whisper transcription happens BEFORE the extractor runs (Phase 25 path), so its latency is not in Phase 38's budget.
- **Cheaper-model routing:** none. Sonnet 4.6 for everything — the continuity decision is small enough that switching to Haiku saves ~$0.005/draft and would force a second model surface to maintain. Revisit if eval shows continuity is reliably easy AND Phase 38 ever has >1000 drafts/day.
- **Caching beyond prompt cache:** none in Phase 38. Drafts are unique-per-capture-set (D-02a id is hash-deterministic) — exact-match cache would only fire on retries of the *same* capture, which the retry-loop handles inline. No semantic cache: extraction outputs are write-once, never re-served to farmers.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### Dimensions

Each rubric is grounded in Section 1b's domain ingredients (Block-naming B5, Stage-event B7 mapping, Lineage C4, Quantity literalism, Confidence calibration, Stage-of-life C1) plus the five Section 1 failure modes (off-schema, confident-wrong, multimodal fusion, lineage break, farmer-facing artifact corruption).

| # | Dimension | Rubric (Pass / Fail in domain language) | Measurement | Priority |
|---|-----------|-----------------------------------------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | **Schema conformance (EXT-01 / C5)** | **PASS:** `tool_use.input` round-trips through `Draft` Zod schema (`.safeParse().success === true`), every key is in the B7 `discriminatedUnion`, no fabricated bundles. **FAIL:** Zod issues non-empty after the single retry, OR any key not declared in the B7 schema is present. | **Code** — `Draft.safeParse(toolUse.input)` in the eval harness; identical to the runtime validator. | **Critical** |
| 2 | **Block-naming compliance (B5)** | **PASS:** `block_name` matches `/^\d{6}_[A-Z]{3}_\d{2,4}$/`, the species trigraph matches the canonical set (SHI/OYS/LIO/REI/...), and the value either equals the CSV ground-truth value OR the field is empty with `_confidence.block_name < 0.7` AND an ask-back fires on it. **FAIL:** Wrong trigraph (`SHII`), invented SEQ, invented date, OR block_name confidently set (`>=0.7`) without ground-truth support. | **Code** — regex + trigraph allow-list + CSV exact-match; ask-back-triggered case scored by harness as PASS. | **Critical** |
| 3 | **Stage-event mapping (B7 log-type selection)** | **PASS:** Emitted `log_type` matches the event class in ground truth (inoculation→seeding, contam→activity:contam, cold-shock→activity:cold_shock, pinning photo→observation, harvest→harvest). Asset.stage is **never** written. **FAIL:** Inoculation written as generic activity/observation; new bundle type invented; harvest recorded as seeding; any field named `stage` on an asset draft. | **Code** — ground-truth `log_type` exact-match from CSV + denylist regex for `asset.stage`. | **Critical** |
| 4 | **Lineage faithfulness (C4)** | **PASS:** For multi-parent events, `parent_block_names` is a set-equal to the ground-truth parent list (order-insensitive). **FAIL:** Any ground-truth parent dropped; collapsed to single parent; reordered into a primary/secondary hierarchy the farmer did not state. | **Code** — set-equality on `parent_block_names` vs CSV ground truth. | **Critical** |
| 5 | **Quantity literalism** | **PASS:** `qty` equals the farmer's literal integer; `weight_per_unit_g` carries the ~estimate with lowered confidence when farmer hedged ("about 250"); no silent unit conversion; "a couple"→`qty=2 conf<0.7`, "a few"→ask-back fires. **FAIL:** Bot multiplies units (3 bags @250g → `qty=750`), converts farmer's units without being asked, or rounds a literal `5` to "about 5". | **Code** — CSV exact-match on `qty`; LLM-judge for hedge-handling on the small subset of cases where ground truth is "approx". | **High** |
| 6 | **Confidence calibration** | **PASS:** Empirical accuracy of fields marked `_confidence >= 0.9` is `>= 90%` across the mushdatadump set; ECE (expected calibration error) across 10 bins `<= 0.10`. Low-quality input (Spanish-English code-switch, blurry photo, ambiguous pronoun) produces appropriately low confidence on the affected fields. **FAIL:** The "0.95 on everything" pathology; high confidence paired with wrong value (the dangerous quadrant); ECE > 0.10. | **Code** — Brier + ECE computed across all dimension-2/3/4/5 field outcomes in the run. | **Critical** |
| 7 | **Ask-back appropriateness** | **PASS:** When ground truth marks a field as ambiguous in the source (handwriting smudge, hedge phrase, dropped pronoun), the bot triggers ask-back on that field rather than guessing — counts as PASS, NOT failure-to-extract (D-06). Conversely, when ground truth is unambiguous, the bot does NOT ask back. **FAIL:** Confident wrong guess on a flagged-ambiguous field; OR ask-back fires on a trivially-clear field (over-cautious flood). | **LLM Judge** — Sonnet 4.6 graded against the CSV's ambiguity flag column; ~10% of judgments human-spot-checked by Don Santiago for calibration drift. | **Critical** |
| 8 | **Multimodal fusion (one event → one draft)** | **PASS:** A capture row carrying text + transcript + photo about a single event produces exactly one `signal_draft` row (verified via `source_capture_ids` array length and `id` determinism). **FAIL:** Two drafts produced for one event; OR two genuinely-distinct events in one voice note silently merged into one draft. | **Code** — count distinct draft ids per source capture; compare against ground-truth event count in CSV. | **High** |
| 9 | **Farmer-facing artifact hygiene** | **PASS:** Every byte the farmer sees in Signal (ask-back text + `[?]`-annotated preview + the "marked for manual review" message) contains zero em-dashes (`—`), zero unrounded floats, and uses `?` / `--` / `n/a` / prose for nulls. **FAIL:** Any em-dash, any `94.3999%`-style float, any LLM-tell punctuation in farmer-facing strings. (Em-dashes in `signal_draft.audit_jsonb` or AI-SPEC.md are out of scope.) | **Code** — regex sweep `[—–]`, `\d+\.\d{2,}%?` over `signal_draft.farmer_facing_preview` and outbound Signal payload. | **High** |
| 10 | **Tool-call discipline** | **PASS:** Every extraction response contains exactly one `tool_use` block with name `submit_extraction`. Continuity calls contain exactly one `decide_continuity` block. No `text`-only responses, no multiple tool calls, no off-name tools. **FAIL:** `text` response when forced tool was requested; multiple tool calls; wrong tool name. | **Code** — harness asserts on raw Anthropic response shape. | **Medium** |

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| **Schema-validation retry** | `Draft.safeParse(toolUse.input).success === false` on the first extraction call. | **Retry once** with a follow-up message carrying `{ type: 'tool_result', tool_use_id, is_error: true, content: "<zod issues>" }`. If the second attempt also fails Zod, write `signal_draft.audit_jsonb.zod_issues`, set `status='needs_review'`. **(Block)** |
| **Off-schema field rejection** | Any key in `tool_use.input` that is not declared in the B7 `discriminatedUnion`. | Reject before any DB write; treat identically to a Zod failure (Block + retry-once). Should be unreachable because `tool_choice` forces shape, but defense-in-depth. **(Block)** |
| **Low-confidence ask-back** | Any required field has `_confidence < EXTRACTION_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD` (default 0.7), OR any required field is unresolved. | Transition draft to `awaiting_farmer`, compose the `[?]`-annotated preview + one-line top question, send via Signal. **(Escalate to farmer)** |
| **Ask-back turn cap** | `signal_draft.ask_back_turns >= 3` (D-05). | Send round-numbered "I can't lock this one, marked for manual review" reply to the farmer; status → `needs_review`; fire a Signal alert to Don Santiago via the existing alerter path. **(Escalate to Don Santiago)** |
| **LLM degraded path** | Anthropic 5xx / 429 / timeout after `maxRetries: 2`. | Fall back to the existing `llm-client.js` degraded-path branch: reply to farmer with `"I will get back to you on this one"` (rounded, no em-dash), and page Don Santiago via Signal alert. **(Escalate)** |
| **Farmer-facing artifact sweep** | Outbound Signal payload contains `[—–]` OR a float with `>=2` decimals OR any other em-dash variant. | **Reject the send**, log the offending payload to `signal_draft.audit_jsonb.style_violation`, compose a replacement via `fmtNum()` + `?`/`--`/`n/a` substitution. If replacement also fails sweep (loop detected), mark `needs_review` and page Don Santiago. **(Block + flag)** |
| **30-min idle gap** | New inbound capture from a sender with an in-flight draft `last_touched > 30min` ago (D-01a). | Force `start-new` regardless of LLM continuity decision; expire the stale draft. **(Block stale-context bleed)** |
| **Per-sender concurrency invariant** | INSERT into `signal_draft` would create a second row with `status IN ('pending','awaiting_farmer')` for the same `sender_e164`. | Partial-unique index raises; orchestrator expires the older draft and retries the insert. **(Block + remediate)** |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Per-dimension pass rate (week-over-week)** | 100% sample during pilot (3 farmers × low volume); downsample later when volume grows. Computed from `signal_draft` rows joined to Phase 39's YES/NO/EDIT signals. | If any **Critical** dimension's pass rate drops more than 5 pp week-over-week, file a `999.*` backlog item and prompt-iterate against the offending cases in the harness. |
| **Confidence calibration drift** | All confirmed drafts (Phase 39 YES) + 100% of EDIT drafts. Bin `_confidence` into deciles, measure empirical accuracy per bin using the farmer's YES/NO/EDIT signal as ground truth. | If the gap between stated and empirical accuracy exceeds **10 pp** in any decile, re-tune the prompt (system + few-shot) OR shift `EXTRACTION_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD`. ECE > 0.10 → blocking review before next ship. |
| **Per-farmer ask-back rate** | Rolling 7-day window per `farmos_person`. | If a farmer's ask-back rate climbs above ~30% sustained, investigate: voice-note quality degraded? Whisper drift? Schema field mismatch with how that farmer actually talks? Surface to Don Santiago + the farmer in question. |
| **Schema-validation-fail rate (post-retry)** | All extractions. | Track per `log_type`. If any type's post-retry fail rate exceeds 2% over rolling 7 days, suspect a B7 schema/prompt mismatch — re-read the farmOS schema lock notes and update the system prompt. |
| **Mushdatadump regression delta** | Re-run `npm run eval:extraction` against mushdatadump on every prompt change OR Sonnet model bump. | Any dimension's pass rate dropping below its prior baseline blocks merge until investigated. Per-case diffs visible in `38-EVAL-RAW.jsonl`. |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** **Existing alerter structured-logger** (`src/agents/alerter/src/log.js` — verify shape during plan-phase; the alerter today uses a pino-style JSON logger to stdout, scraped by the host journal). **No new tracing infra.** **Not Arize Phoenix** — Phoenix's value (multi-framework OTel span aggregation, vector-DB inspection, RAG-specific views) is overkill for one extractor call against a known schema; the alerter's logs already aggregate everything we need and are queryable via the existing host-side log pipeline.

Every extraction call emits one structured log line at INFO with the following fields:

| Field | Source |
|-------|--------|
| `draft_id` | `sha256(sort(source_capture_ids))` — D-02a |
| `sender_e164` | `signal_capture.sender_e164` |
| `farmos_person` | `signal_capture.farmos_person` (Phase 37) |
| `n_input_tokens` | `msg.usage.input_tokens` |
| `n_output_tokens` | `msg.usage.output_tokens` |
| `n_cache_read_tokens` | `msg.usage.cache_read_input_tokens` (cache-hit telemetry) |
| `cost_estimate_usd` | Computed from token counts × pricing constants |
| `latency_ms` | wall-clock around `messages.create()` |
| `continuity_decision` | `append` / `replace` / `start-new` from D-01 |
| `per_field_confidence_min` | `min(Object.values(draft._confidence))` |
| `schema_validation_result` | `ok` / `retry_succeeded` / `needs_review` |
| `ask_back_turn` | `signal_draft.ask_back_turns` post-write |
| `log_type` | extracted `draft.log_type` (cardinality-bounded: 5 values) |

The Phase 39 farmer-confirmation signal (YES/NO/EDIT) is joined back to these log lines via `draft_id` for the calibration flywheel.

**Key Metrics to Track:**

1. **Draft-confirmation rate** (Phase 39 YES / total drafts) — **north-star**; the single number that says "is this product working for farmers?"
2. **Schema-validation-fail rate** (post-retry) — operational health of the LLM↔Zod contract.
3. **Mean ask-back turns to completion** — measures how often the bot resolves on turn 1 vs. drags farmers through 2–3 turns. Targets `<= 1.5`.
4. **Per-draft Anthropic cost (USD)** — derived from `n_input_tokens`/`n_output_tokens`/`n_cache_read_tokens`; rolling 24h mean.
5. **p95 extraction latency** — wall-clock for the extraction call alone (continuity + ask-back composition tracked separately).

**Alert Thresholds** (page Don Santiago via the existing Signal alerter path):

| Metric | Threshold | Page? |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Draft-confirmation rate (rolling 7-day) | drops below 70% | **Yes** — north-star regression |
| Schema-validation-fail rate (rolling 24h) | exceeds 5% | **Yes** — operational |
| p95 extraction latency | greater than 30s | **Yes** — farmer-perceived UX |
| Anthropic spend (rolling 24h total) | exceeds `$10/day` | **Early-warning, not paging** — log a Signal note to Don Santiago, not a page |
| Per-farmer ask-back rate (rolling 7-day) | exceeds 30% for any pilot farmer | **Yes** — that farmer is being bookkeeping-taxed (NORTH-STAR violation) |
| Ask-back-cap (`needs_review`) rate | exceeds 10% of drafts in any 24h | **Yes** — bot is getting stuck systematically |

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**

- **100% review** of `needs_review` drafts (these already escalated; Don Santiago walks each one).
- **100% review** of EDIT-confirmed drafts from Phase 39 — EDITs are the cheapest signal of "model got it almost right" and the highest-leverage source for prompt iteration. Don Santiago plus the farmer who made the edit see each one in the weekly review.
- **Random 10% sample** of YES-confirmed drafts — these go into the confidence-calibration flywheel (Section 6); without sampling YES drafts the calibration baseline goes blind.
- **0% review** of NO-discarded drafts unless the farmer flagged them with a specific reason — discard signal alone is too noisy without farmer context.
- **All adversarial / unusual inputs** (drafts where any `_confidence` field is below 0.5, OR where the LLM-judge ask-back-appropriateness score disagrees with the deterministic confidence check) automatically routed to the weekly review queue regardless of the YES/NO/EDIT outcome.
