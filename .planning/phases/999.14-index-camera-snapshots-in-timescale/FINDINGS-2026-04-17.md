# Camera history findings — 2026-04-17

Captured from a farmer debug session ("camera down? i see black on MC").
Two distinct usability issues surfaced. Both block the stated 999.14 goal
("farmer can browse chamber history without SSH") and both are bigger than
the original snapshot-indexing framing.

---

## Issue 1 — fc_camera idle-mode stall (Phase 12 regression)

**Symptom:** Mission Control showed a black/frozen image. Bridge snapshot
history showed the *same* file size (32095 bytes) every 15 min for 7+ hours.

**Root cause:** fc_camera logged a transition to idle at 13:53:14 (0.000278
fps) and never transitioned back to active, even when the bridge subscribed
on the farmer's viewer connect. Bridge was re-saving a stale cached frame
every snapshot tick because no new frames were being published.

**Hypothesis:** `get_subscription_count()` polling on the fc_camera side
isn't correctly detecting the bridge's post-idle subscription, *or* the
idle timer stopped capturing entirely after the first transition. Needs
a proper investigation.

**Workaround applied today:** `ssh fc1-ts 'sudo systemctl restart fc-core'`.
Camera recovered within ~10s; first fresh snapshot after restart was 32374
bytes (different size = new frame).

**Severity:** high. A farmer checking the chamber sees a frozen image and
can't tell whether the camera, the stream, or the chamber is the problem.
The `status` indicator doesn't differentiate "live" from "stuck".

**Followup:** deserves its own bug-fix phase (not a backlog item — this is
a Phase 12 regression in shipped v1.2 code). Suggest filing as a decimal
phase against the current milestone rather than piling onto 999.14.

**Canonical files:**
- `src/chambers/fc-core/fc_core/fc_camera.py` — subscriber polling logic
- `src/chambers/fc-core/fc_core/test/test_camera.py` — test harness
- `.planning/phases/12-subscriber-aware-camera/` — original design

## Issue 2 — idle pulse is never persisted (design flaw in 999.14 framing)

**Discovery:** When the farmer asked "are the idle-rate frames saved too?",
tracing the bridge code revealed they aren't.

**Mechanism:**
- Phase 12 made the bridge *subscriber-aware* to fc_camera: it calls
  `ensureCameraSubscribed()` only when an MJPEG client connects
  (`src/mission-control/bridge/src/index.js:73`) and
  `maybeCameraUnsubscribe()` when the last client disconnects (line 86).
- The 15-min snapshot timer (`setInterval(saveSnapshot, ...)`, line 381)
  writes whatever `latestFrame` is.
- With zero viewers, the bridge isn't subscribed, so `latestFrame` doesn't
  update. fc_camera's 1-frame-per-hour idle pulse is published to the ROS
  topic but the bridge isn't listening — the frame is discarded.

**Implication:** The only history we have is the window when *someone was
actively watching Mission Control*. Overnight, weekends, any stretch of
"nobody's looking" = zero frames persisted. The 4G-thrift win of Phase 12
came at the cost of historical continuity, and we didn't notice because
nothing in the stack surfaced the gap.

**What this changes about 999.14 scope:**

The original backlog note framed 999.14 as "just index the files the bridge
already saves." That's wrong. Indexing discontinuous-when-unwatched files
gives the farmer a timeline scrubber with mostly blank hours. Real scope:

1. **Decide who persists idle frames.** Options:
   - Bridge stays subscribed at a trickle rate even with zero viewers
     (reintroduces a small 4G cost — quantify: 1 frame/hr × ~32KB
     ≈ 0.8 MB/day, probably fine vs. the 35 MB/day of the pre-Phase-12
     workaround).
   - Persistence moves to the Pi side (fc_camera writes a local history
     ring buffer, syncs to elder-plops when reachable — composes with
     999.1 edge-buffering).
   - A second "archivist" subscriber on elder-plops that only persists,
     never streams. Decouples viewer-presence from history-presence.
2. **Index in Timescale** (the original 999.14 goal) — now covers a
   continuous timeline.
3. **MC timeline scrubber UI** — so the farmer can actually use it.

**Decision needed from the farmer/operator before planning:** acceptable
idle persistence rate (1/hr? 1/15min? 1/5min?) and where the cost lives
(4G budget vs. Pi storage vs. complexity).

## Also noticed (not blocking, but relevant)

- Bridge has no "frame freshness" signal. `/health` reports
  `camera.subscribed: true|false` but not "last frame age". A stall like
  today would be invisible to any monitor.
- fc_camera's idle transition log line fires once; the recovery transition
  (back to active) didn't fire at all today. Logs alone can't distinguish
  "healthy idle" from "stuck idle" — needs a heartbeat.
