<objective>
Wiring: light up T4 (commit_success) + T6 (commit_failed) terminal-state acks end-to-end. This is the plan where user-visible behavior changes. Plans 01+02+03 are dormant until this plan ships.

Sequence per dispatch:
1. commit-watchdog post-T4 / post-T6: call `commitDb.tryMarkOutcomeAckSent(pool, draftId)`.
2. If claim returns ok=false, exit silently (another tick/process won, or already sent).
3. If claim wins, call `ctx.outboundConfirm.dispatch('send_commit_outcome_ack', row, {outcome, reason?})`.
4. outbound-confirm switch: imports renderOutcomeAck, builds body, sends via safeSend.
5. Best-effort log to signal_outbound (intent='commit_outcome_ack', tenant_id='mossrock', related_draft_id=row.id). On log error: warn, do not unwind ack.

Purpose: closes the NORTH-STAR violation for all future commits. The two named historical drafts get acked in Plan 05.
Output: terminal-state acks shipping in prod next deploy.
</objective>
