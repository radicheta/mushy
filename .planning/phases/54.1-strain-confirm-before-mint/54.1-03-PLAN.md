<objective>
Hook the per-encounter strain ask-back into the Phase 39 confirm-loop. When a live capture
yields an unknown strain (per Plan 01's resolveStrain against config.strains), the farmer
gets a single ask-back: "Hey, I saw 'XYZ' on today's log -- new strain, or did you mean
<nearest>? Confirm or correct." On YES, the draft's commit is authorized to mint the term
(createMissingFungiType=true for that draft only); on a correction, the draft strain is
remapped to the canonical code and nothing is minted.

Purpose: This is the live counterpart to Plan 02's batched backfill confirm. It guarantees
`ensureFungiTypeUuid(create=true)` is reached in the live path ONLY after a farmer YES --
the live commit-watchdog's shared ctx keeps createMissingFungiType false by default, and
only a per-draft farmer-confirmed marker flips it on for that one commit.

Output: a `strain-ask-back.js` render+parse module, a `send_strain_ask_back` side-effect in
outbound-confirm.js, and the receive-loop YES/correction handling that authorizes the mint
or remaps the code. Jest coverage proves no-mint-without-YES.
</objective>
