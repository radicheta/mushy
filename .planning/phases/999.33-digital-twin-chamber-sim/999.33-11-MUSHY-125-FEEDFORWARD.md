## Reading

* **Shape.** A constant duty-per-(C/h) gain is the wrong form: the model gain
  (rh*V*dAH_sat/dT - C)/F is -0.16 at 3.6 C, +0.11 at 10.6 C, +0.39 at 16 C,
  +0.72 at 20 C. Below ~6 C the wet surfaces re-evaporate MORE than the air
  needs and RH rises through a warming ramp; a winter-sized constant would
  be inert in spring and a spring-sized one wrong in winter. The kernel
  therefore computes the gain from (rh, T) and exposes one trim.
* **Winter days: real but small, as the MUSHY-62 audit predicted.**
  2026-08-08 (3.6-10.6 C): rms 1.534 -> 1.506 at trim 1.0, inside noise.
  2026-08-14 (7-16 C, the active held-out day): rms 2.809 -> 2.655, min
  86.53 -> 87.47, in-band 31% -> 48%, mean duty 0.175 -> 0.161 (it holds RH
  with LESS water, because it stops over-correcting after the ramp). Relay
  edges +0.8/h. Trim 1.0 is the best or second-best row on both days; 2.0
  is worse on both. Filter tau 300-1200 s changes nothing that matters.
* **Spring, where the farmer saw the let-go: the twin cannot say.**
  2026-05-04 baseline (trim 0) already has rms 4.82 / min 84 / max 99.4 /
  mean duty 0.62 against recorded 1.58 / 93.3 / 96.6 / 0.43: at a 96%
  target the fitted F starves the twin into bypass for most of the day
  (MUSHY-137 regime, RH near the saturation exclusion). The feedforward
  changes nothing there because the loop is pinned at full duty anyway.
  This day is the one the ticket nominates and the one where the term
  should matter most; the evidence has to come from the chamber, not the
  twin.
* **Safety of trim 1.0 on the twin.** RH max never rises on any day (the
  duty_bias_factor fade holds), relay wear +0.3..+2.5 edges/h, no runaway,
  no extra water. Nothing here argues against a live trial; nothing here
  proves the payoff either. Same discipline as the slew limiter: measured,
  not assumed.

## Recommendation

Ship the mechanism default OFF (`humidifier_temp_feedforward: 0.0`). When
the farmer is present for a dawn ramp, set trim 1.0 as a runtime param
(`ros2 param set`, same as the bias) and compare the morning against the
previous one at the same target. The interesting test is the first warm
spell (>14 C daytime), not another winter day. If the real chamber ramps
as the model says, trim stays 1.0; if RH overshoots on warming, halve it.

Reproduce: `.venv/bin/python scripts/sweep-temp-feedforward.py` (needs the
timescale container for the 2026-05-04 export; Aug days are cached).
