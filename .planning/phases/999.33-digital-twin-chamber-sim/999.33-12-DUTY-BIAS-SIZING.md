Reading: on the quiet in-band day the bias does what it was designed for --
rms 1.24 -> 0.83, in-band 83 -> 97%, the sag below midpoint gone. On days the
twin already over-swings (its MUSHY-137 residual: 08-08/14/29 max 94-95 vs
recorded 92.6-94.9) the bias costs 0.03-0.16 rms because it lifts the mid-band
operating point; the fade caps the damage (max moves <= 0.5 pp). Relay edges
+1..+3/h, i.e. 80-150/day, under the 180/day MUSHY-116 target. The sigma-delta
driver has already removed half B (discard), so the bias is now a quiet-day
refinement, not the cycle-killer it looked like against the 120 s window driver.

## Recommendation

`humidifier_duty_bias: 0.12`. Under the model's 0.176 and the recorded 0.20 on
purpose; best or within noise on the quiet days; worst case on a swinging day
is +0.16 rms. NOT set in config (default 0.0 stays): it has never executed on
the chamber, so it goes in as a runtime param on an attended day
(`ros2 param set /fc_controller humidifier_duty_bias 0.12`), then into
fc_config.yaml once a quiet 24 h looks like the 08-24 row. Abort condition:
RH sitting above band_high with the humidifier still pulsing.
