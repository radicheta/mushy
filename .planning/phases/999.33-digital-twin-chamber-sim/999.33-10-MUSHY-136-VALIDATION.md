# 999.33-10 -- Surface reservoir term: refit and validation (MUSHY-136)

Follows the MUSHY-62 audit (`audit-MUSHY-62/AUDIT.md`), which found that the
chamber's absolute humidity on an idle night tracks the saturation curve at
near-constant RH, and that both earlier Q estimates were cooling-rate
artefacts. This phase makes the term real and refits.

## Model

    V * dAH/dt = F * u  -  Q * (AH_in - AH_out)  +  C * dT/dt

`C` is `ChamberParams.surface_g_per_k`: grams of water the chamber air
gains per kelvin of chamber warming (loses per kelvin of cooling) through
reversible exchange with wet walls, substrate and standing water. Linear in
dT/dt; no RH-level dependence (see "Limits").

## Refit (`scripts/fit-chamber-model.py`, window 2026-04-11..2026-08-08)

Quiet-band regression now solves the 2x2 normal equations for (Q, C); the F
regression removes both loss terms; the day-block bootstrap and the
validate/fit ratio carry C. The script's own primary-vs-aggregated estimator
cross-check passed.

## Limits, stated

* **The active day still over-swings ~2x** (12.3 vs 6.2 pp on 2026-08-14)
  and under-delivers water (0.71x). C fixed the sign and most of the
  magnitude (32 pp before); what is left is in the active regime, where the
  humidifier's mist also lands on the same surfaces. The dT/dt form has no
  way to absorb an excess above surface equilibrium.
* **A chamber pinned at 97% with the humidifier off drifts +1.7 pp wet** over
  a day (2026-08-18). Same cause: the surfaces hold RH at an equilibrium,
  which a relaxation `-k (AH - RH_s * AH_sat(T))` would express and dT/dt
  does not. Upgrade path noted in `ChamberParams`.
* The constant-temperature synthetic gate (`test_replay_fidelity.py`) cannot
  exercise C; its amplitude assertions are xfail with that reason. The
  driven replays above are the amplitude gate.
* C was fitted at 85-99% RH on a chamber kept wet; a dry chamber has nothing
  to re-evaporate.
* Ambient is still the reanalysis grid cell ~4 km away (MUSHY-67); the three
  held-out windows use the `.recent` side fixture (forecast-grade for its
  last days).
